Where I start. Rated myself 0–4 on twenty skills; the numbers are on the [progress page](/progress/).

**What I can do:** open chords without looking, power chords and bar chords so the E and A strings are familiar, one pentatonic box (the first one everyone learns), play through a song if the chart is in front of me.

**What goes wrong on Sunday:** lead lines aren't clean, I hit other strings. Fingers aren't fast enough. Pinky cramps if a song sits on bar chords too long. Guitar ends up in the back seat, not heard.

**What I want by 31 Dec:** rhythm electric that can build energy at the peak of a hymn and play one clean lead line. Same notes, different strings, and the song lifts.

**Setup:** HX Stomp, bought patches, can't build my own yet. 30 minutes, five days a week, living room, sitting. Never recorded myself.

**Week 1 starts with hands, not theory:** muting and metronome picking. [Week 1 drill](/drills/week-01/).
